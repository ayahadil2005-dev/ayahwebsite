About Me

Hello, my name is Ayah Adil Babiker Mohamed, and I am from Khartoum, Sudan. I am a simple girl with big dreams, and I always try to make my family proud of me.

My Personality

I am a quiet, organized, and responsible person. I love to plan my day and finish my work on time. I do not like to leave things for the last moment, because I feel comfortable when everything is arranged.

I am also a patient person. When something becomes difficult, I do not give up easily. I try again and again until I understand it and find a solution. I believe that every problem has a solution if we stay calm and keep trying.

Another thing about me is that I am a very honest person. I love to say the truth and I love to be clear with the people around me. I think honesty makes life easier and makes relationships stronger.

My Family

My family is the most important part of my life. I live with a lovely family that always supports me and encourages me to continue my education and to become a better person.

I love to spend time with my family. I feel safe and happy when I am with them. They are the first people I share my good news with, and they are the first people who stand beside me when I feel sad or tired.

My family taught me to be respectful, kind, and responsible. They taught me to love learning and to work hard for my future. Everything I am today is because of their love and support.

My Way With People

I love to be with my friends. I am not a person who talks a lot, but I am a good listener. When my friends need someone to listen to them, I am always there for them.

I believe that kindness is very important in life. I love to help the people around me, and I feel happy when I can make someone smile or make their day easier. Even a small kind word can change someone's day.

I also love to learn from other people. Every person has a different story and a different experience, and I enjoy listening to their stories and learning something new from them.

My Dreams

My dream is not only to be successful in my studies, but also to be a good person who helps her family and her society. I want to be a strong and independent woman who can depend on herself and achieve her goals.

Thank you for reading about me. I hope this page helped you to know my real personality, not only as a student, but as a person.
